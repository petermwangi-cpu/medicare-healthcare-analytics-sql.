# Healthcare Analytics Business Questions

## Patient Analytics
1. How many patients are registered?
2. What is the gender distribution?
3. Which cities have the highest patient populations?

## Clinical Analytics
1. What are the most common diagnoses?
2. Which patients have critical conditions?
3. What is the average hospital stay duration?

## Operational Analytics
1. Which doctors handle the highest workload?
2. What percentage of appointments are completed?
3. Which visit types are most common?

## Financial Analytics
1. What is total hospital revenue?
2. Which services generate the highest income?
3. Which patients have the highest healthcare costs?
